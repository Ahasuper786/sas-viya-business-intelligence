# Data and Project Scope

## Source material

The portfolio is derived from the author's 2026 SAS for Business Intelligence coursework PDF. Screenshots were extracted directly from that document. They have not been redrawn or altered to change the displayed results.

## Measures and interpretation

- **Order Total:** the order-value measure displayed in the Insight Toys report. It is not described here as profit or as revenue generated by the author.
- **Profit:** the measure displayed in the Orion Star product-group and time-series visuals.
- **Gross Profit Margin:** the country-level percentage measure in the Insight Toys report. The underlying formula cannot be inspected without the SAS report definition, so this case study does not claim to have independently recalculated it.
- **Number of Orders:** a count measure displayed in the Orion Star report.

No new numerical estimates are inferred from chart pixels. Findings are limited to the rankings and patterns visible in the screenshots and described in the original report.

## Authorship and context

- Orion Star: the dashboard was supplied for evaluation. The author's contribution was analysis, interpretation and discussion of its interactions.
- Insight Toys: the author created the two-page report in SAS Viya and explained the reporting choices.
- Both are academic business scenarios, not paid consulting engagements or production deployments.

## Reproducibility

This repository includes static evidence and written analysis. The source SAS datasets, a data licence for redistribution and native editable report files were not part of the supplied PDF. They are not included, and there is no executable SAS program to run from this repository.
